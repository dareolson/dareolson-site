About

Derek Olson covered crime and politics as a reporter and studied at the Poynter Institute before he picked up a camera. He spent a decade in Los Angeles as a stage manager at Smashbox Studios and production supervisor at Disney's Maker Studios, and he shot extreme sports at the Sochi Winter Olympics.

He earned a full-stack web development certificate from UCLA Extension in 2017. He works in JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node, Python, and Postgres, and he built and runs The Rate Guide, a day-rate platform for freelance film crews. See his builds.

He runs Grain and Thunder now, out of North Dakota.
