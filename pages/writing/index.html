About the writer

Derek Olson covered crime and politics as a reporter and studied at the Poynter Institute before he picked up a camera. His reporting won a Los Angeles Press Club honorable mention and a Nevada Press Association first place for feature writing. He is a filmmaker and developer in Minot, North Dakota.
